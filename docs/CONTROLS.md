# Controls

## Camera
- **WASD / arrow keys**: pan
- **Screen edges**: pan (while the game window has focus)
- **Mouse wheel**: zoom
- **Right mouse drag**: grab and pan the map
- **Minimap**: left click or drag moves the camera

## Selecting
- **Left click**: select a unit or building of your faction
- **Left drag**: box-select units
- **Shift + click / drag**: add to the selection

## Orders (units selected)
- **Right click ground**: move
- **Right click enemy unit or building**: attack it
- **Right click deposit**: harvesters gather
- **Right click unfinished building**: workers help build it
- **Ctrl + right click**: attack-move (fight whatever is met on the way)
- **Right click on the minimap**: same orders, anywhere on the map

## Command card (bottom right) and number keys
- With a worker selected: buttons or **1, 2, 3** pick a building, then **left click** places it, **right click or Esc** cancels
- With a production building selected: buttons or **1, 2** queue a unit, **Shift + number** queues a Plan batch (Collectivists)

## Winning
Destroy the enemy headquarters. Lose yours and you lose.
